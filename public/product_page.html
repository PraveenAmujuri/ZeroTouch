Slip a LOZ in your pocket, purse or carry-on and take a mouth-watering refresher wherever the day goes. Our sugar-free, Moisturizing Mint lozenges deliver a refreshing, thirst-quenching feel that lasts. Developed with a periodontist and maxillofacial surgeon, LOZ pairs a clean spearmint taste that freshens breath with a smooth, velvety mouth-feel for everyday comfort.

Every LOZ is produced in the United States with high quality ingredients:

  • Sodium Hyaluronate: A moisture-binding ingredient studied for supporting gum health and moisturizing soft tissues in the mouth
  • Birch-Derived Xylitol: A non-GMO, sugar-free ingredient widely used in oral care that supports a balanced oral microbiome
  • Bee Propolis Extract: A natural protectant long valued for supporting the health of teeth and gums
  • Sunflower-Derived d-alpha-tocopherol: A premium, soy-free antioxidant that keeps every LOZ fresh

Directions

Dissolve one lozenge in mouth up to two times daily or use as directed by a healthcare professional.

Ingredients

Isomalt (non-GMO), Xylitol (Organic Birch Derived), Sodium Hyaluronate, Bee Propolis Extract, Natural Spearmint Flavor, d-alpha-tocopherol (Sunflower Derived), Pectin (Apple Derived), Fruit Juice Color (Huito)

Warnings & Considerations

Precaution: Consult with a physician if you are pregnant or nursing, taking medications or have a medical condition. 


Allergy Warning: Propolis can cause severe allergic reactions. If irritation or swelling to the throat or mouth occurs, discontinue use and seek immediate medical assistance. 

Keep out of reach of children. 

Contains xylitol. Keep out of reach of pets.
